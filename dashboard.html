Devices & Drivers

Read-only · Local

Discover connected and installed Windows devices, driver versions and devices reporting errors. Use the guided Driver Repair Assistant to review trusted update paths. PC Medic does not automatically modify drivers.

Click Scan Devices to check your Windows hardware. Results stay on this PC. Device inventory is separate from the main health check.
How to update a driver safely

Use Windows Settings → Windows Update → Advanced options → Optional updates, or Device Manager. For a specific device, obtain the driver from your PC or device manufacturer's official support site. Create a restore point before significant driver changes. No driver changes are made by PC Medic.
